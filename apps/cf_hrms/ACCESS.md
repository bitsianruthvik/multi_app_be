| Named right | What it is |
|---|---|
| `cf_hrms_employee_create` | Create an employee. The appointment step of a hiring: it creates the employee and issues the employee code. |
| `cf_hrms_employee_close` | Close an employee. Start, change, cancel and close a person’s leaving. |
| `cf_hrms_employee_bulk_add` | Add employees from Excel. The Excel upload that adds employees and skips hiring. |
| `cf_hrms_contact_view` | See contact details. Phone, email and address, and statutory identifiers in full. |
| `cf_hrms_pay_view` | See pay. CTC and everything made from it: the hiring terms, the letters and their signed copies. |
| `cf_hrms_access_manage` | Manage logins and access. Create logins, change what each access role may do, disable a login, reset a password. |
| `cf_hrms_self_view` | See their own place. A person’s own position, managers, team and responsibilities — and the team rule of a manager. |

How to read the tables below:
- **a or b** — holding either is enough (a picker two screens share).
- **and** — both are needed.
- **scope: the position / the person / the department** — for a login whose access role is “only their department and below”, the thing named must be inside it (403 `OUT_OF_SCOPE`). A whole-company login is not affected.
- **whole-company logins only** — the thing belongs to no department, so a department-scoped login cannot change it.
- `cf_hrms_contact_view` and `cf_hrms_pay_view` also change what a route RETURNS: without them contact details and pay are removed from the answer on the server.
- **filters: …** — the search and filters that list takes in its address (`?q=&status=`); it answers `total` and `count` beside its rows.
- `upload` rights guard the Excel uploads (the Excel section).
